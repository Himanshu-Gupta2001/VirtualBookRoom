Weekly Contact Deletion Automation - Recent DE Sync

An investigation was conducted following incident INC4950155 to determine the cause of recurring discrepancies between Salesforce Lead Object counts and Salesforce Marketing Cloud (SFMC) Synchronized Data Extension (DE) counts. Analysis identified that when Salesforce Leads are converted to Contacts, the lead receives a new Contact ID but continues to exist in the Salesforce Lead Object and corresponding synchronized Lead DE.

The existing Weekly Contact Deletion automation identifies orphaned lead records using only the _ContactKey field. As a result, converted leads are incorrectly classified as orphaned and deleted from SFMC synchronized data extensions. During the next synchronization cycle, these records are repopulated from Salesforce, causing recurring data discrepancies and necessitating frequent DE refreshes.

To resolve this issue, the deletion logic must be updated to validate against both _ContactKey and ID in Lead_Salesforce_5, ensuring converted leads are excluded from deletion processing. Validation demonstrates that this change reduces false-positive deletions by approximately 75.6%, preventing valid converted leads from being removed.

------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------

AC1: Weekly Contact Deletion Query Updated
The "COLLECT — Lead Orphans (00Q% ContactKey)" query within the Weekly Contact Deletion automation is updated to:
Continue excluding records where Lead_Salesforce_5._ContactKey = ContactKey.
Additionally exclude records where Lead_Salesforce_5.ID = ContactKey.

AC2: Converted Leads Are Not Flagged for Deletion
Converted Lead records that continue to exist in Salesforce and the synchronized Lead DE are not identified as orphan records.
Records whose ContactKey matches a valid Lead ID are retained.

AC3: Query Validation Completed
The revised query is executed against production data.
Validation confirms that converted lead records are excluded from the deletion candidate population.

AC4: Expected Record Reduction Achieved
The revised query reduces the deletion candidate population from approximately 77,828 records to approximately 18,986 records.
Approximately 58,842 converted leads are excluded from deletion.
Reduction is approximately 75.6%.

AC5: Automation Deployment
Weekly Contact Deletion automation is paused during implementation.
Updated query is deployed.
Automation is re-enabled following successful validation.

AC6: Post-Deployment Monitoring
Subsequent synchronization cycles are monitored.
No recurring discrepancy attributable to converted-lead deletions is observed.
Reconciliation between Salesforce Lead Object counts and SFMC Synchronized DE counts shows expected improvement.

AC7: Business Outcome
Converted leads remain available in synchronized data extensions.
Frequent manual synchronized DE refresh requests are no longer required to correct count discrepancies.
