Investigate and remediate remaining Lead/Contact synchronization discrepancies related to the contact deletion process

As the technical product manager, I want to investigate and resolve the remaining lead/contact synchronization discrepancies between Salesforce and Marketing Cloud following execution of the contact deletion process, so that synchronized data extensions remain aligned, data integrity is maintained, and recurring synchronization incidents are prevented.

Business Value

Improves data integrity between Salesforce and Marketing Cloud

Reduces recurring operational incidents and support requests

Eliminates manual synchronization efforts and approvals

Minimizes risk associated with future deployments and data refreshes

Improves confidence in lead and contact synchronization processes

-------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------

SCENARIO 1: Identify remaining synchronization discrepancies
GIVEN there are discrepancies in lead and contact record counts after the contact deletion process is completed
WHEN lead and contact synchronization results are analyzed
THEN all remaining out-of-sync record populations are identified and documented
AND the total affected record counts are captured
AND potential root-cause categories are documented
AND required changes to the contact deletion automation are identified and implemented

SCENARIO 2: Validate synchronization results
GIVEN the approved remediation has been implemented
WHEN synchronization and data refresh processes are executed
AND the updated contact deletion automation has ran
THEN lead and contact record counts align within established tolerances // what is the acceptable threshold?
AND previously identified out-of-sync records are resolved
